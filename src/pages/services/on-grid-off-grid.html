The right kind of solar system for your site

On-grid vs off-grid at a glance

Most homes and businesses with a reliable grid connection are best served by on-grid solar. Off-grid makes sense where the grid is unreliable, expensive to extend, or not available.

FeatureOn-grid systemOff-grid system
How it worksConnected to the electricity grid. Solar power is used on site first; any surplus can be exported where metering rules allow.Independent of the grid. Batteries store solar energy for use at night and on cloudy days.
BatteriesNot required.Required, and need periodic maintenance and eventual replacement.
During a power cutSwitches off automatically for safety.Keeps supplying power from the batteries.
Upfront costLower for the same capacity.Higher, because of batteries and charge control.
Best forHomes, businesses and industries with a reliable grid that want to reduce electricity bills.Remote sites, farms and premises with frequent outages or no grid connection.

Need both? Hybrid systems combine a grid connection with battery backup. Ask us whether a hybrid design suits your site.

Components & quality

Components specified to recognised standards

A system is only as reliable as its weakest component. We specify equipment tested to recognised international and Indian standards, such as:

  • PV modulesIEC 61215
  • InvertersIEC 61683 / IEC 60068
  • MPPT charge controllersIEC 62093 / IEC 60068
  • BatteriesIEC 61427 / IS 1651 / IS 13369
  • CablesIEC 60189 / IS 694 / IS 1554
  • Switches & circuit breakersIS / IEC 60947 / EN 50521
  • Junction boxesIP65 outdoor / IP21 indoor

Built for the long term

What a well-designed system gives you

  • Long service life. Quality solar modules are designed for around 25 years of operation.
  • Low maintenance. No moving parts in the panels; regular cleaning and periodic checks keep output up.
  • Monitoring. Inverter and plant monitoring show how your system is performing.
  • Backup on cloudy days. With batteries, off-grid and hybrid systems keep supplying power when the sun doesn't.
  • Genuine spares. We use and supply genuine replacement parts for service.
