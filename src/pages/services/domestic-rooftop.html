What's included

A simple process, from home visit to switch-on

  1. Home visit and roof check

    We look at your roof, its direction and any shading, and go through your electricity bills with you.

  2. A clear recommendation

    We explain the right size and type of system for your home, and what it will cost, in plain language.

  3. Neat, safe installation

    Our team installs the panels, inverter and wiring with proper earthing and protection.

  4. Connection and after-sales service

    We help with the connection steps and stay available if you need service later.
