What we do

O&M services

Small problems cost generation every day they go unnoticed. Our service is built around finding and fixing them early.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled inspection and maintenance of modules, structures, inverters, cabling and protection equipment.

Module cleaning

Regular cleaning to a schedule that suits your site's dust and weather conditions.

Periodic health checks

Plant health checks to spot degradation, hot spots, loose connections and wear before they become failures.

Monitoring, analytics & reporting

Real-time performance data and regular reports, so you can see how your plant is performing.

O&M manuals

Clear operations and maintenance documentation for your plant and your on-site team.

Corrective maintenance & spares

Fault response and repairs using genuine spare parts.
