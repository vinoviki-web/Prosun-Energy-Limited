Commercial & industrial solar

Use your roof to cut daytime power costs

Factories, mills, hospitals, colleges and commercial buildings use most of their electricity during the day, which is exactly when solar generates. A rooftop or captive plant designed around your load can reduce how much you buy from the grid.

Request a Site Assessment See C&I projects

Our approach

Sized for your business, not a brochure

A plant that's too small leaves savings on the table. One that's too large can export power you get little value for. We size carefully.

Load and bill analysis

We study your electricity bills, sanctioned load and operating hours to understand when and how you use power.

Roof and structure check

We assess the roof type, condition, orientation and shading, and design mounting structures to suit metal-sheet or RCC roofs.

Metering and connection

Rules for net and gross metering vary by consumer category and capacity. We explain what applies to you and design accordingly.

Safe, planned installation

Installation is sequenced around your operations, with safe access, cable routing and protection designed in.

Commissioning and handover

Testing, commissioning and support through inspection and grid connection, with documentation for your team.

Ongoing O&M

Cleaning, preventive maintenance and performance reporting to protect your investment over the long term.

Getting started

What to have ready for your first conversation

You don't need everything below to get in touch, but having it on hand helps us give you a useful answer quickly.

  • Your last 12 months of electricity bills
  • Sanctioned or contracted load
  • Roof drawings, or a few photos of the roof
  • Your typical operating hours and days
  • Site address
