Solar EPC & industrial projects

Solar power plants, built properly the first time

For industries, developers and investors who need a dependable partner. Prosun delivers turnkey engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) for ground-mounted and large rooftop plants, and project management consulting for utility-scale developments.

Scope of work

Everything from feasibility to start-up

Take the full turnkey package, or bring us in for the stages where you need support.

Feasibility & site studies

Land or roof assessment, solar resource, shading, access and grid connectivity, so the project starts on firm ground.

Pre-bid & detailed engineering

Plant layouts, structural and electrical design, single-line diagrams and equipment specifications.

Procurement

Modules, inverters, mounting structures, cables, switchgear and transformers, sourced to specification and delivered to schedule.

Construction

Civil, mechanical and electrical works, managed on site with a focus on safety and workmanship.

Testing, commissioning & start-up

Pre-commissioning checks, commissioning and support through grid synchronisation and start-up.

Project management consulting

Owner-side project management, pre-bid and design engineering for developers running their own EPC process.

Who it's for

A partner for developers, industries and investors

  • Manufacturers and process industries that want captive solar to offset daytime grid power.
  • Project developers and IPPs who need an EPC contractor or an owner-side project management consultant.
  • Investors who want engineering diligence and dependable execution.
  • EPC contractors that need engineering or site execution support.

Project capability

We deliver solar power projects from several hundred kilowatts up to 100 MW.
