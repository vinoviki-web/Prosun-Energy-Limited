Privacy

How we use the information you share with us through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, phone number, and optionally your email address, location, project type, approximate system size or electricity bill, and any message.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, for example to call you back, arrange a site visit or send you a proposal. We do not sell your details or share them with other companies for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to manage any project or service that follows.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time by emailing info@psun.in or calling 1800 102 3317.
