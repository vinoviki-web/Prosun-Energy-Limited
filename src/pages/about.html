About Prosun

Engineering-led solar delivery from Coimbatore

Prosun Energy Pvt Ltd is a professionally managed solar company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We deliver complete solar power projects: concept, detailed engineering, construction, commissioning, start-up and operations & maintenance support.

Our story

Two decades in solar, from components to complete power plants

Prosun was established in 2003 to raise the standard of renewable energy projects through in-depth engineering expertise and hands-on field experience.

We started with the design, supply and installation of solar photovoltaic systems, and Prosun is an approved vendor of TEDA (Tamil Nadu Energy Development Agency). Today we take on complete projects, from a few kilowatts on a home rooftop to multi-megawatt plants, and we have the capability to deliver projects of up to 100 MW.

Since 2017, Prosun has collaborated with ACE Renewtech on national and international solar projects, including project management consulting for NLC India Limited's solar plants in Tirunelveli district.
