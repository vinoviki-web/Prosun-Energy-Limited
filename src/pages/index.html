How a project works

From first conversation to a plant that keeps performing

You deal with one team throughout, so nothing gets lost between design, installation and maintenance.

  1. Site assessment

    We review your electricity bills and sanctioned load, then visit to check the roof or land, shading and grid connection.

  2. Design & engineering

    We size the system and prepare layouts and electrical designs. You get a clear proposal with expected generation and costs.

  3. Procurement

    We source modules, inverters, mounting structures and electrical components matched to the design and your budget.

  4. Installation & commissioning

    Our team installs, tests and commissions the system safely, and supports the grid-connection and inspection steps.

  5. Ongoing support

    After handover, our O&M team can monitor, clean and maintain the plant so it keeps generating as designed.
